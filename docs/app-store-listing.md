## Description (4,000)

> **Chat with people nearby — no internet, no signal, no account.**
>
> HelloLetter sends messages phone to phone over Bluetooth. Messages hop through other phones running HelloLetter, so a group spread along a trail, across a campsite or through a crowd can stay in touch when there are no bars at all.
>
> **Made for when the network isn't there**
> • #nearby: one group chat with everyone in range
> • Private messages, end-to-end encrypted: phones that pass them on can't read them
> • Envelopes: if your friend is out of reach, your message is sealed and carried by people you pass, and delivered when one of them meets your friend
> • Nothing missed: messages wait up to 3 days for someone out of range, and #nearby catches you up on the last 12 hours when you come back
> • Alerts with your phone locked, without showing names or text unless you choose to
>
> **Private by design**
> • No accounts, no phone number, no email
> • No servers: we never see your messages
> • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
> • Verify friends in person with a safety number or QR code
> • Block, report, hide offensive words, a privacy screen, and Erase everything
>
> **Reach people without the app**
> Send a one-way message over AirDrop, which also works offline, or invite friends to get HelloLetter before your trip.
>
> Tip: everyone in your group needs HelloLetter installed before you set off.

## App privacy (the "nutrition label")

**Data Not Collected.** HelloLetter has no server and sends nothing to the developer. A report email is sent by the user from their own Mail app, only if they choose to.

## Review notes (for App Review)

> HelloLetter is an offline, peer-to-peer messenger. It sends messages over Bluetooth LE between nearby iPhones running HelloLetter; there is no server and no account. With a single device you'll see #nearby empty with an explanation of how it works. The attached video shows two iPhones chatting with Wi-Fi and mobile data off.
>
> Guideline 1.2 (user-generated content):
> • Filter: offensive words are masked on the device, on by default (Settings → Help and safety).
> • Report: touch and hold a message → Report… blocks the sender immediately on the device and offers an email report to our support address.
> • Block: on a person's page, or through Report.
> • Contact: Settings → Help and safety, and the support URL.
> Because there is no server, content never passes through us, and blocking takes effect on the reporter's phone at once.
>
> Background modes: bluetooth-central and bluetooth-peripheral keep the mesh reachable with the phone locked; this is the app's core function.
>
> Encryption: private messages use the Noise protocol (standard algorithms).

Attach a 30–60 second video: two phones in airplane mode with Bluetooth on, sending in #nearby, a private message with its ticks, and Report → Block.
